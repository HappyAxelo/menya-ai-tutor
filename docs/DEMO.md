## What this shows, and what it does not

- The problems, reference answers and marking are deterministic; the misconception labels come from the wrong answers they produce, not from the model's guess.
- After a guided solve, the learner gets an unseen problem and solves it without help; only that counts as independent evidence. Mastery would additionally need a later success after a break of at least two days (spaced review), which a single session cannot show.
- The student's turns are scripted. This demonstrates the system's behaviour, not learning gains: those need the study in docs/EVALUATION_PLAN.md.
