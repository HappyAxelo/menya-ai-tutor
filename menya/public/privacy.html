MENYA AI TUTOR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Menya AI Tutor ("Menya", "we") is an online tutor that helps learners understand problems step by step. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Menya at menyaneza.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier. When you sign in with an email link, we receive your email address. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Your tutoring conversations. The questions you ask, your answers and the tutor's replies, so you can continue a problem and review past sessions.
  • Learning and usage information. Your plan, how many messages and hints you use, the topics the tutor detects, and technical records of each request (time, success or failure, and response time). These records do not contain your messages.
  • Preferences stored on your device. Settings such as language, theme and text size are kept in your browser and are not sent to us.
  • Institution membership. If your university or school gives you access, we record which institution you belong to and whether you have a seat.

How we use it

  • To provide tutoring: generating replies, keeping your conversation history and tracking hints.
  • To apply your plan's limits fairly and prevent abuse.
  • To keep the service reliable and to understand costs and usage in aggregate.
  • For institutions you belong to: to show your institution's administrators aggregate information, such as how many learners are active and which topics are common. Administrators cannot read your conversations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers that process your information

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions (data stored in the European Union).
  • Groq and, for some advanced requests, Anthropic process the text of your tutoring messages to generate replies. They receive the conversation text needed for the reply, not your email address.
  • Voice notes and photos: when you record your voice or attach a photo, image or audio file, it is sent to Groq to be turned into text (speech recognition, reading the picture). Only the resulting text is kept in your conversation; the recording or picture itself is not stored by Menya. Documents (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text and code files) are read on your own device, and only their text is sent with your message. For Kinyarwanda, your browser's own speech recognition may be used, which is provided by your browser's maker (for example Google in Chrome).
  • Google provides sign-in when you choose "Continue with Google".
  • GitHub Pages serves the website.

These providers process information on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

How long we keep it

  • Tutoring messages are deleted automatically after 180 days.
  • Your account information is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, conversations and memberships are deleted. Usage counts needed for billing records are kept without your identity.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: in Menya, open your account and choose "Download my data".
  • Delete your account: in Menya, open your account and choose "Delete my account".
  • Delete a conversation: from your recent sessions.
  • You can also email us to access, correct or delete your information.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each person can only access their own conversations and records. Access keys for AI providers are kept on our servers and are never sent to your browser.

Children

Menya is designed for secondary-school, university and adult learners. If you are under the age required to consent to online services in your country, please use Menya with the permission of a parent, guardian or your school.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
